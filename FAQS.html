FAQ's

What is life Bridge?
Life Bridge is a platform designed to connect organ donors with individuals who need organ transplants.
How does Life Bridge work?
Life Bridge provides information about organ donation and allows interested donors and recipients to express their interest thought the platform. It helps facilitate connections while prioritizing safety, privacy and medical. guidance
Who can use life bridge?
Anyone interested in learning about organ donation, registering their interest in becoming a donor, or seeking information about organ transplantation can use Life Bridge
What is organ donation
Organ donation is the process of giving an organ or tissue to another person who needs it because of organ failure or serious medical condition.
How can someone become an organ recipient?
Someone who needs an organ transplant should first consult a qualified medical professional or transplant center. They will assess the patient's condition and determine whether transplantation is appropriate
Does registering on Life Bridge guarantee that I will receive an organ?
No.Registering on Life Bridge does not guarantee an organ transplant. Organ allocation depends on medical compatibility, urgency, clinic assessments and applicable transplant regulations
Can someone sell or buy an organ through Life Bridge?
No. Life Bridge doesn't support the buying or selling of human organs. Organ donation must follow applicable laws and ethical medical standards
