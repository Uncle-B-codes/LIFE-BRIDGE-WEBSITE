Who Can Donate?

Donation is a voluntary, life-saving act. Most healthy adults can donate-here's what you need to know.

Check the eligibility checklist below and clear up common myths before you decide.

Check Eligibilty

Eligibilty Checklist

Age 18+

You must be atleast 18 years old to donate.

Good Health

Donars should be in good general health at the time of donation.

Voluntary Decision

Donation must be your own free, informed choice.

Myths vs Facts

Myth

"Doctors won't save me if iam a donor."

Fact

Doctors always save you first-donation is only considered after all life-saving efforts have failed.

Myth

"My religion forbids it."

Fact

Most major religions permit and support organ and tissue donation as an act of saving life.
